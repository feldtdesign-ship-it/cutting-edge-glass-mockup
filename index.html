Dover Sportsplex

Dover, NJ · Commercial renovation

A 1955 supermarket, closed up for decades. The glass is what opened it again.

It went up in 1955 as a Food Fair supermarket on Bassett Highway, then served as the NJ Department of Labor and later a candy and tobacco warehouse.

West Morris Capital bought it in 2021 and reopened it in late 2022. The concrete arch had been filled with tinted panels and read as a closed wall. Clear glass from the sidewalk to the crown turned the courts into the facade. At night the whole arch glows.

Fratelli Beretta USA

Flanders, NJ · Industrial

A food plant set into the forest, its offices wrapped in cedar and glass. Our scope: the front entrance.

The plant covers about 215,000 square feet on a steel frame at the Mount Olive International Trade Center. The production hall is wrapped in dark panels. The offices stand apart in vertical cedar, their glass set behind a screen of wood fins that turns the facade into a lantern at dusk.

Red Bull Training Facility

East Hanover, NJ · SportSample copy

A training ground for a top-flight soccer club. The glass had to take a stray ball and still give the coaches a clear line to every pitch.

The New York Red Bulls have trained in East Hanover since 2013. When the club added a 4,000 square foot academy building in 2015, the brief was simple: let the players see the fields from the locker rooms, and let the coaches see in.

We glazed the entrance and the field-facing wall in laminated, tempered glass, then ran frameless partitions through the treatment and weight rooms. The building opened in time for the spring season.

Story is sample copy

Jason Scharfstein opened Cutting Edge Glass in Rockaway in 1998 with one truck, a rented bench and a rule he still keeps: measure every opening yourself. The first jobs were frameless showers for local builders. Within a few years those builders were asking for storefronts and office glass, and the shop grew to meet them.

His brother Greg joined to run operations, and the business moved to 14 Eastmans Road in Parsippany, with room for a full cutting and polishing floor. Today the brothers lead a crew that glazes curtain wall, entrances and structural glass across the tri-state area and beyond, and still builds showers at njshowerdoors.com. One of them is on every job, from the first measure to the final clean.
